Uncle Joe's Roofing is named for my great-great-uncle Joe. He was the first Catholic Bishop of Allentown.

Uncle Joe had a beach house on Long Beach Island. He left it to my grandmother. The house had yellow shutters with white anchors cut into them. Over the years it became a local landmark. People on the island knew it as "the Uncle Joe house."

The anchor in our logo comes from those shutters. So does the yellow.

That house stood for something simple: family you can count on. That's what we want to be for your home.

Everybody needs an uncle who knows roofs. The one you call when you want a straight answer. The one who tells you what your roof needs — and what it doesn't. The one who won't steer you wrong.

That's us. We're a roofing-only company based in Yardley. We're owner-led, we work with local crews, and we price our work fairly. No storm-chasing. No pressure. Just good roofs, done right, for our neighbors.

So if you've got a roof question, call your uncle.
