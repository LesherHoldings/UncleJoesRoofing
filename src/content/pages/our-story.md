Uncle Joe's Roofing is named for my great-great-uncle Joe. He was a priest — the first Catholic Bishop of Allentown — and he had a small house on Long Beach Island.

It wasn't a fancy place. What people remembered were the shutters: painted yellow, with a white anchor cut into each one. Folks on the island knew it as "the Uncle Joe house." After he passed it stayed in the family, and it stayed the same plain, steady little house it had always been.

The anchor in our logo comes from those shutters. So does the yellow.

Uncle Joe stood for something simple: show up, tell the truth, and look after people. That's the kind of company we're trying to run.

Everybody needs an uncle who knows roofs. The one you call when you want a straight answer. The one who tells you what your roof needs — and what it doesn't. The one who won't steer you wrong.

That's us. We're a roofing-only company based in Blue Bell, working in Conshohocken, Malvern, Paoli and the towns around them. We're owner-led, we work with local crews, and we price our work fairly. No storm-chasing. No pressure. Just good roofs, done right, for our neighbors.

One note on the fine print: on paperwork you'll see Fortress Siding. That's the company Uncle Joe's Roofing operates under — same owner, same office. We gave the roofing work its own name because that's all it does.

So if you've got a roof question, call your uncle.
