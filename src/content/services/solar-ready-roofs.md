## Why it matters

Solar panels are typically expected to last for decades. If the roof underneath is already halfway through its life, you could end up paying to remove and reinstall panels just to replace the shingles. Doing the roof first means one job, done once.

## What "solar-ready" means with us

It's still a roof first. We install a new asphalt shingle roof the same careful way we always do. Then we pay extra attention to the things that matter for solar later: a solid deck, a clean layout on the sunniest slopes, and good records of what's up there.

You choose the solar company and the timing. We just make sure the roof won't be the weak link.
