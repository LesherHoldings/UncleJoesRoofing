## Bigger roofs, more details

Many Blue Bell homes have complex rooflines: several gables, valleys where slopes meet, dormers, and additions tied into the main house. Every one of those is a place where water can get in if the flashing isn't done right. That's where we slow down.

## Roofs coming due

Neighborhoods here went up in waves, so roofs on the same street tend to wear out around the same time. If your neighbors are getting new roofs, it's a good time to have yours checked — not because you have to replace it, but so you know where you stand.

## Ventilation matters

Larger two-story homes often have big attics. If the attic can't breathe, heat and moisture build up and shorten the life of your shingles. We check intake and exhaust ventilation on every replacement and fix what's out of balance.

## Neat and quiet

These are well-kept neighborhoods. We tarp landscaping, keep the driveway clear, sweep for nails and leave things the way we found them.
