## Ice dams, valleys and big attics: Blue Bell's winter roof problems

Southeastern Pennsylvania winters freeze and thaw over and over. Meltwater refreezes at the eaves and in the valleys, and a multi-gable colonial has plenty of valleys for an ice dam. Two things keep it in check: ice and water shield at the eaves and valleys, and an attic that can breathe. Big two-story homes have big attics, and poor venting shortens shingle life, so we check ventilation on every replacement. Summer brings the opposite problem: algae streaks on shaded slopes. [How a roof quote is built](/pricing-and-warranties/) explains what goes into the price of a repair or a replacement.
