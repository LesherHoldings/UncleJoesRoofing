## Older homes on the hill

Many of Conshohocken's homes went up when it was a mill town. When we tear off an old roof here, we often find layers of shingles, old board sheathing and flashing that's been patched more than once. We strip it all the way down, replace any bad boards and build the roof back up properly.

## Twins and rowhomes

Attached homes share more than a wall. The roof line, flashing and gutters often run across both houses. That's where leaks like to start. We pay extra attention to the joint between your roof and your neighbor's, and we'll keep your neighbor in the loop if work affects their side.

## Townhomes and HOAs

The newer communities near the river usually have HOA rules about shingle style and color. We'll check those with you before you choose, and help you get anything the association needs to approve the work.

## Tight streets, clean jobs

Narrow streets and steep grades take planning. We schedule deliveries carefully, protect walkways and neighboring cars, and sweep for nails before we leave each day.
