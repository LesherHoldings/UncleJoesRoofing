## Roofers in King of Prussia, PA 19406

King of Prussia is the commercial center of Upper Merion Township in Montgomery County, but away from the mall and the office parks it has its share of ordinary houses: ranches, split-levels and colonials from the 1950s through the 1970s off Henderson Road and Route 202, and newer townhomes near the mall and Valley Forge. We're a roofing-only contractor based in Blue Bell, and the owner lives in the Conshohocken area, so King of Prussia roofing is close to home. If you're comparing King of Prussia roofers, ask each one for a Pennsylvania home improvement contractor registration number; ours is PA203681.

## Wind, highways and open ground

Wind is the roofing story here. Roofs near the highways and the open ground around Valley Forge tend to have less shelter than the older tree-lined streets, and the first thing to go in a storm is usually a run of shingles along the ridge or a piece of flashing at a chimney. If you've found shingle pieces in the yard, a [free roof inspection](/free-roof-inspection/) with photos is the calm way to find out what, if anything, needs doing.

## Repair or replace in Upper Merion

A home built in the 1950s through the 1970s has usually had at least one new roof already. If that roof went on over the original shingles, it can't be done again: a third layer isn't an option, and the deck under two layers has often never been seen. That means a full [roof replacement](/roof-replacement/), with everything stripped to the deck first. When the shingles are sound and the trouble is one chimney or one valley, a [roof repair](/roof-repair/) is the better use of the money, and we'll say which it is after we've photographed the roof.
