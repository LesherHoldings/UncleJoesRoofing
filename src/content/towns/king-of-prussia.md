## Roofers in King of Prussia, PA

Wind is the roofing story in King of Prussia. Open stretches near the highways and the park funnel gusts across rooftops, and the first thing to go is usually a run of shingles along the ridge or a piece of flashing at a chimney. If you've found shingle pieces in the yard after a storm, a [free roof inspection](/free-roof-inspection/) with photos is the calm way to find out what, if anything, needs doing.

## Second roofs coming due

Many Upper Merion homes went up in the 1950s through the 1970s and got their first replacement in the '90s or 2000s. Some of those were layered over the original shingles, which can't be done again. When that second roof wears out, it needs a full [roof replacement](/roof-replacement/) down to the deck. If the problem is one spot, a [roof repair](/roof-repair/) done right is the honest answer, and we'll tell you which it is.
