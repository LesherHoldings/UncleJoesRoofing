## Older homes near the center

Homes around the middle of the borough have been standing a long time. When we replace a roof here, we expect to find old boards, previous repairs and sometimes more than one layer of shingles. We take it all off and fix the deck before the new roof goes on.

## Ranches and split-levels

A lot of Langhorne-area homes have lower-pitched roofs and several separate sections. Low-pitch roofs need the right underlayment to keep water out, especially at the edges and where sections meet. That's a detail we don't skip.

## Not every Langhorne address is in the borough

The Langhorne mailing address stretches well beyond the borough itself, into Langhorne Manor and parts of Middletown Township. Wherever your Langhorne address is, we'll come out.

## Straight answers

If your roof just needs a repair, we'll tell you. If it's time for a new one, we'll explain why and show you the photos.
