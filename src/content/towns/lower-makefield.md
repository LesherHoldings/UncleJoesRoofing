## Roofs that are coming due

A lot of Lower Makefield neighborhoods went up in waves. When one house on the street needs a roof, the neighbors usually aren't far behind. If your neighbors are getting new roofs, it's a good time to have yours looked at — not because you have to replace it, but so you know where you stand.

## Bigger roofs, more details

Many homes here have complex rooflines: several gables, valleys where slopes meet, skylights, and additions tied into the main roof. Every one of those is a place where water can find its way in if the flashing isn't done right. That's where we slow down.

## Ventilation matters

Larger two-story homes often have big attics. If the attic doesn't breathe, heat and moisture build up and shorten the life of your shingles. We check intake and exhaust ventilation on every replacement and fix what's out of balance.

## Neat and quiet

These are well-kept streets. We tarp your landscaping, keep the driveway clear, sweep for nails and leave things the way we found them.
