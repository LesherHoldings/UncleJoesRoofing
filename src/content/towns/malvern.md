## Older homes in the borough

The streets around King Street have some of the oldest homes in the area. Steep roofs, dormers and old chimneys all need careful flashing. When we tear off, we expect to find old boards and earlier repairs, and we fix the deck before the new roof goes on.

## Newer neighborhoods around it

The townships around Malvern are full of homes from the last few decades. Many of those roofs are on their first set of shingles and getting close to replacement. A free inspection will tell you how much life yours has left.

## Townhomes and HOAs

If you're in a townhome community, the new roof has to tie into the units next to yours and meet your HOA's rules. We plan for both before we start.

## Straight answers

If your roof just needs a repair, we'll tell you. If it's time for a new one, we'll explain why and show you the photos.
