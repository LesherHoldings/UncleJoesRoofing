## Homes that have grown over time

Many Levittown-section homes in Middletown started small and grew. Additions, dormers and enclosed porches all bring new roof sections, and each one has to tie into the original roof. Those seams are where leaks often start. We pay close attention to them.

## Know your pitch

Some additions were built with flatter roofs than the main house. Asphalt shingles need a certain amount of slope to work. If part of your roof is too flat for shingles, we'll tell you straight — that's better than a roof that leaks in two years.

## Lots of mailing addresses, one township

Depending on your street, your mail may say Langhorne, Levittown or another town name. If you're in Middletown Township, we cover you.

One quote, one person in charge, local crews and a clean yard when we're done. That's how we do every job, whatever size the house.
