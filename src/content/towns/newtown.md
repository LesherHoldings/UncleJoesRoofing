## Historic homes in the borough

The streets around State Street and the historic Newtown Theatre are some of the oldest in Bucks County. Roofs here are part of the look of the town. Before we quote, we'll talk with you about whether your home falls under the borough's historic review process, so there are no surprises.

## Townhomes and HOAs in the township

Newtown Township has many planned communities and townhome rows. Roofing one unit in a row takes care: the new roof has to tie cleanly into the roofs on either side, and the shingles have to meet HOA rules. We plan for both before we start.

## Single-family neighborhoods

Many township neighborhoods were built in the 1980s and 1990s. A lot of those roofs are on their second set of shingles or due for one. A free inspection will tell you where yours stands.

## Easy to reach

Newtown is a short drive from our home base in Yardley, so scheduling inspections and follow-ups is easy.
