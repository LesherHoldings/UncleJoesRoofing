## Stone homes and chimneys

Many Paoli homes are built of stone, with tall chimneys and steep roofs. Where a roof meets a stone wall or chimney is one of the most common places for a leak. We replace that flashing the right way — cut into the mortar, not just caulked on.

## Mid-century homes

Ranches and split-levels often have lower-pitched roofs and several separate sections. Low-pitch roofs need the right underlayment to keep water out, especially at the edges and where sections meet. That's a detail we don't skip.

## Honest about slate

The Main Line has plenty of slate roofs. We're asphalt shingle specialists. If you have slate that's still in good shape, we'll say so — and we won't sell you a roof you don't need.
