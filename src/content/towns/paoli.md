## Stone homes and chimneys

Paoli has its share of stone and stucco colonials from the 1920s through the 1950s, with tall chimneys and steep roofs. Where a roof meets a stone wall or chimney is one of the most common places for a leak. We replace that flashing the right way: cut into the mortar, not just caulked on.

## Mid-century homes

Ranches and split-levels often have lower-pitched roofs and several separate sections. Low-pitch roofs need the right underlayment to keep water out, especially at the edges and where sections meet. That's a detail we don't skip, whether it's one section or a [full roof replacement in Paoli](/roof-replacement/).

## Freeze-thaw and stone chimneys: the Main Line's usual leak

A Main Line winter freezes and thaws for months. Water works in behind the flashing at a chimney or stone wall, freezes, and pries the joint open a little wider each time. Low-pitch ranch sections hold snow and ice longer, so ice at the eaves is the other winter problem, and the old trees along the Main Line add moss and algae streaks in summer. When the deck and the shingles are still sound, [new chimney flashing](/roof-repair/) is usually all the roof needs. A [free roof inspection in Paoli](/free-roof-inspection/) with photos tells you whether that's your case or the whole roof is due.
