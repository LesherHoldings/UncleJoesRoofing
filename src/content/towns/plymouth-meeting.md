## Roofs built in waves

Neighborhoods in Plymouth Meeting, PA 19462, went up in waves, so roofs on the same street tend to wear out around the same time. When the neighbors start getting new roofs, it's worth a [free roof inspection](/free-roof-inspection/), not because you have to replace yours, but so you know where you stand before a storm decides for you.

## Ranches, split-levels and the low-slope problem

A lot of homes here have a low-slope section over a garage, a breezeway or a rear addition tied into a steeper main roof. Those transitions are where leaks start. On a [roof replacement](/roof-replacement/) we run ice and water shield across the low sections and rebuild the flashing where the slopes meet. If the main roof has life left, a targeted [roof repair](/roof-repair/) at the transition can buy years.
