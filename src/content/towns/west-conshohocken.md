## Roofing in a hillside borough

West Conshohocken, PA 19428, is small, but the roofs vary a lot: tall, narrow twins and rowhomes on the hill, porch roofs and flat rear sections, and newer townhomes near the Schuylkill with tighter access. Wind funnels up the river valley, so lifted shingles and loose flashing are common calls. If you're seeing stains on an upstairs ceiling or shingle grit in the gutters, a [free roof inspection](/free-roof-inspection/) with photos will tell you where you stand.

## Repair or replace

On older twins we often find roofs with a second layer of shingles over the first. That's a sign a full [roof replacement](/roof-replacement/) is coming, because a third layer isn't an option and the deck underneath has usually never been checked. If the roof is newer and the problem is one spot, a [roof repair](/roof-repair/) done right is the honest answer, and we'll say so.
