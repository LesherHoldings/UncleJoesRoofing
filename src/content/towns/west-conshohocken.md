## Roofing in a hillside borough

West Conshohocken, PA 19428, is a small Montgomery County borough, but the roofs vary a lot: tall, narrow twins and rowhomes on the hill, porch roofs and flat rear sections, and newer townhomes near the Schuylkill with tighter access. Wind funnels up the river valley, so lifted shingles and loose flashing are the usual trouble. If you're seeing stains on an upstairs ceiling or shingle grit in the gutters, a [free roof inspection](/free-roof-inspection/) with photos will tell you where you stand.

## Repair or replace

Older twins here often carry two layers of shingles, and the deck under them has usually never been seen. That calls for a full [roof replacement](/roof-replacement/): tear-off to the deck, replace the soft boards, new flashing at the party line. A newer roof with one bad spot gets a [roof repair](/roof-repair/) and nothing more. We're not going to sell a hillside twin a roof it doesn't need.
