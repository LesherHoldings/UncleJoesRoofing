## Roofing for an old river town

A lot of Yardley's homes were built long before anyone thought about modern roofing. That shows up when we tear off an old roof. We often find multiple layers of shingles, old board sheathing and flashing that's been patched more than once.

That's not a problem. It just means the job has to be done carefully. We strip it all the way down, replace any boards that are soft or split, and build the roof back up properly.

## Trees, shade and moss

Yardley's big old trees are part of its charm. They also drop branches, clog gutters and keep north-facing slopes damp. That can lead to moss, rot at the edges and faster wear. When we inspect, we look closely at shaded slopes and the edges of the roof where water backs up.

## River weather

Being right on the Delaware means wind coming up the river valley and plenty of damp days. Good ventilation and solid flashing matter here. We check both on every roof.
